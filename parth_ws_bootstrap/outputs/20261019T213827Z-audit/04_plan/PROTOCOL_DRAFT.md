## 5. Robustness protocol

Corruption families, physically interpretable units, severities, raw-input insertion points, seeds/realizations, train-only scale estimates and label preservation: UNDECIDED. Historical values are descriptive evidence, not adopted defaults. Begin conceptually with clean plus one degraded modality and its clean counterpart; known absence, both absent/abstention and undetected failure are distinct. Recompute features and quality after corruption; do not corrupt a stale cached representation while claiming a raw-input fault.

Paired held-out sample identity must stay fixed across models/conditions. Clean-trained evaluation freezes weights/calibrators/thresholds; training augmentation is a distinct study. No oracle fault label/severity or assessment-label quality information. Training exposure must be matched to isolate adaptation. ## 6. Outcomes and interpretation

Proposed primary metric family: failure-class average precision as a rank statistic, with its exact convention/prevalence/reporting rule TO LOCK; do not conflate AP and trapezoidal PR area. Primary contrast/aggregation and clean non-inferiority margin: UNDECIDED. Also report absolute corrupted scores, clean-to-corrupted changes, recall/thresholded metrics, calibration and cost. Threshold/calibration provenance must be training-only. Uncertainty unit is a verified independent group, candidate recording; paired comparisons, training/realization variance, seeds/resamples/multiplicity and stop rules are UNDECIDED.

A weak clean model can lose less; smaller degradation alone is insufficient. Gate motion or effective coefficients are not useful adaptivity by themselves or causal percentages. Prespecify all outcomes; keep deteriorations/inconclusive results and avoid selected favorable conditions. A failed validity check blocks a scientific conclusion rather than becoming a negative result.
