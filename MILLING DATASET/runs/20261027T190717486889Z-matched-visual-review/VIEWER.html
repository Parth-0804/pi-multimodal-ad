Matching limitation: every pair shares its experiment and all recorded numerical settings, tool category and material. Cut direction differs in all five pairs: normal DOWN, abnormal UP. This can confound visual differences. The ROI is an approximate tool/workpiece context, not a defect annotation. Enlargement adds no information hidden by coolant or droplets.
Positions match relative locations inside each cut; they are not simultaneous events. Arrow keys step frames. Playback is a slideshow, not original-time video.
