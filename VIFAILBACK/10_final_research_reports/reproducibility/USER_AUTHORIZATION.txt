11. TEST ADAPTIVE FUSION ROBUSTNESS
========================================================

This is central to the thesis.

Evaluate clean data and controlled degradation.

Visual corruption:
- Missing frames
- Frame drops
- Blur
- Occlusion
- Camera unavailable
- Reduced image quality
- Temporal misalignment where validly simulated

Sensor corruption:
- Missing channels
- Missing intervals
- Added noise
- Frozen sensor readings
- Channel dropout
- Controlled temporal shifts

Combined corruption:
- Degraded visual and sensor streams together
- One modality missing completely
- Both modalities partially degraded

Use documented corruption intensities.

Generate corruptions without consulting evaluation labels.

Do not fit corruption severity or gates on held-out results.

Compare:

- Best visual-only model
- Best sensor-only model
- Averaging
- F2
- Quality-aware fusion

fallback behavior.

A hard-coded surviving-modality fallback is not evidence
that a learned gate recognized modality reliability.
