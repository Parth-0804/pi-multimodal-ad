\section{Target Lineage and Pairing}

The PHM target \nolinkurl{phm2026_image_damage_v3} uses one deterministically selected
view for each of teeth 5--28 and aggregates the three highest tooth values for each run.
This gives a provisional image-derived proxy for physical deterioration. Earlier targets
used different image geometry, view aggregation and tooth sets, which limits comparison
of their errors. Sorting tooth values removes positional identity, so the profile
describes their distribution within a run. The fixed image split and the
leave-one-experiment-out sensor evaluation assess different populations.\ev{PHM target
lineage and model mapping}{\EvidenceRef{E021}{}, \EvidenceRef{E108}{}}

The REASSEMBLE release reports 4,551 demonstrations. The analysis included 4,530
completed pick, insert, remove and place actions after applying the primary-action and
input-completeness criteria. Of these, 509 were failures. Each action supplied 22 scalar
sensor channels and a descriptor from 16 hand-camera frames. The core experiment
excluded audio because the correspondence between annotation times and waveform times
could not be independently established.\ev{REASSEMBLE cohort and
gates}{\EvidenceRef{E042}{}, \EvidenceRef{E039}{}}

\section{External Cohorts and Exclusions}

MSM's primary target is intervention-worthy abnormal behaviour, native label 1, against
labels 0 and 2. Label 2 describes stable cutting with a defective tool, rather than
simply belonging to the same semantic class as label 1. A sensitivity excluding 125
label 2 intervals gives 1770 observations. The 45 nominal experiment groups, seven
machine/date proxies and five calendar dates are not interchangeable grouping
identities; the date-level evidence has very few independent clusters. Technical
exclusions establish a usable cohort, not a claim that the excluded intervals were
uninformative.\ev{MSM admitted cohort and target semantics}{\EvidenceRef{E073}{},
\EvidenceRef{E072}{}, \EvidenceRef{E067}{}}

ViFailback contains 5,202 native episodes, with 4,545 failures and 657 successes. Two
camera-quality exclusions reduced a provisional cohort of 4,384 episodes to 4,382. The
820 excluded or quarantined episodes count each episode once even when several exclusion
reasons apply. The historical and later matched CNN conditions assess the same 4,382
episodes under different fitting procedures. Repeated seeds and outer folds reuse these
episodes.\ev{External dataset reconciliation and current result
boundaries}{\EvidenceRef{E106}{}, \EvidenceRef{E107}{}}

The HANDOVER study included 587 of 589 native trials. In two training trials the nearest
wrench observation exceeded the 0.1~s alignment limit. The 151 test trials contain 110
failures and 41 successes. The higher failure prevalence than in REASSEMBLE affects
comparisons of failure-class average precision. Eligibility depended on the native
partition and availability of both modalities. Participant-level independence could not
be verified for individual trials.
