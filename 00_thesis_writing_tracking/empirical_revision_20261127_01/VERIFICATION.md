## What was actually verified

**VERIFIED — saved-score arithmetic, not training reproduction.** [REASSEMBLE arithmetic](reassemble_arithmetic.json) independently recomputes pooled failure-positive average precision, Brier score and fifteen-bin equal-width ECE from saved arrays for clean inputs, all seven strongest corruptions and both absence conditions. Thirty-two model rows agree with the saved summary to maximum absolute error `6.106226635438361e-16`. Archive hashes match their companion metadata; ordered labels and fold vectors agree across conditions (4,530 actions, 509 failures). The check does not independently reconstruct recording identities or repeat bootstrap intervals. The [stdlib implementation](verify_saved_robustness.py) includes small synthetic metric checks and does not import model code.

**VERIFIED — bounded aggregation and transcription.** The [table-input receipt](verified_table_inputs.json) records re-averaging all sixty MSM clean-summary rows from their three saved pooled seed APs and the nine in-sample visual fit APs. It also records the two nineteen-permutation plus-one calculations, each 0.05. [Numeric transcription checks](table_transcription_checks.json) compare 164 numeric cells in 36 newly added rows against that receipt. They do not independently regenerate the underlying MSM or ViFailback probabilities. The heterogeneous REASSEMBLE follow-up table was checked against retained reports and comparison records; its intervals were not newly bootstrapped.

**VERIFIED — source/report correspondence within the inspected files.** PHM diagnostic additions were checked against retained metric tables and dated reports, including the [fixed-split summary](../../implementations/PHM2026/results/runs/phm2026_fusion_latefusion/20260912T054016807597Z-e1911d4f/reports/late_fusion_summary.json), [target/lineage review](../../implementations/PHM2026/docs/studies/THESIS_CODE_TRACEABILITY.md), and [four-arm report](../../implementations/PHM2026/docs/studies/SENSOR_REPRESENTATION_FUSION_RESULTS.md). Existing source-provenance limitations and earlier reviews' verification dates were not relabeled as newly reproduced training. [Source hashes](source_manifest.json) identify the bounded evidence and implementation files retained for this revision; a hash alone does not validate their scientific claims.

**REPORTED — ViFailback fitted calibration and completed controls.** The [matched calibration report](../../implementations/VIFAILBACK/experiments/matched_cnn_20_100_f2_20261111/14_final_research_reports/08_STATIC_SENSOR_AND_F2_RESULTS.md) reports negative visual slopes for outer fold 0 in all FROZEN20/DONOR20 seeds and FULL20 seed 42. Current inspected source implements `sigmoid(a * failure_logit + b)` and passes calibrated `probability` columns to F2. This establishes the implementation's sign convention and input path. Individual fitted calibrators and keyed matched prediction files were unavailable locally, so this revision cannot newly verify each executed fit or reconstruct the reported AP values. Negative slopes are not labeled an implementation defect, and the evidence does not establish the signs of all inner-fit or sensor calibrators. Earlier November verification retains its own date and access scope.

**VERIFIED — chronology of retained documents; UNKNOWN — human inspection order.** The dated 29 October snapshot already lists original visual, high-camera, numerical and clean-fusion results before the 30 October reporting clarification. This supports disclosure of assessment availability, not a claim that the study was prospectively preregistered or that the researcher's precise inspection sequence has been reconstructed.

No model training, inference, preprocessing, calibration fitting, new corruption experiment or environment installation was performed. Raw data, run artifacts, checkpoints and existing processes were not changed. No new literature references were introduced or claimed independently revalidated in this source revision.
