## PADERBORN: preserve the disputed protocol history

The [architecture validation report](../../implementations/PADERBORN/docs/ARCHITECTURE_VALIDATION_RESULTS.md) reports a large change from stratified-window to bearing-held-out performance. Its stricter vibration MLP comparison gives MAE difference −0.1184 [−0.2978, 0.0589] versus the constant, whereas the requested condition-only comparison gives −0.2754 [−0.4404, −0.1126]. The latter allows the same bearings across conditions. The report promoted the stricter bearing-and-condition definition as primary; this is a documented protocol fork, not automatically a formally approved amendment. Both conclusions must retain their own grouping.

The reported bearing-identity prediction (53.8% versus 3.6% chance) makes identity-related signal plausible when severity is constant per bearing. It does not prove all target prediction is an identity shortcut. The stricter inconclusive interval is not evidence of equivalence or “no information.” This is exactly why the omitted study matters: independence requirements can change what a seemingly strong architecture result means.

## ViFailback: specific amendment after the manuscript cutoff

The [24 November completed-scope report](../../VIFAILBACK/experiments/matched_cnn_20_100_f2_20261111/18_fit_stop_and_analysis_20261124T1955Z/completed_scope_analysis/RESULT_ANALYSIS.md) and [final status](../../VIFAILBACK/experiments/matched_cnn_20_100_f2_20261111/18_fit_stop_and_analysis_20261124T1955Z/FINAL_STATUS.json) supersede the draft's 74-fit/deferred-only description:

| Condition | Completed / planned fits | Assessment scope |
|---|---:|---|
| FROZEN20 | 36/36 | Three seeds, three outer folds and their three-inner banks |
| DONOR20 | 36/36 | Same coverage; source-trained representation, new target components |
| FULL20 | 4/36 | Seed 42, outer fold 0: outer-final plus three inner fits |
| FROZEN100 / FULL100 / DONOR100 | 1/1 each | Separate single-bank pilot protocol, same 1,107 assessment episodes |

Total: 79 complete, 32 unfinished of 111 planned fits. Completion at the researcher-imposed stopping boundary does not mean completion of the original matrix. The report's own limits paragraph still says “when the current inner2 fit completes”; the timestamped final status confirms it completed. Use the final receipt and explicit coverage for that status correction, retaining the stale sentence as a documentary inconsistency rather than silently adopting it.

For seed 42, fold 0, FULL20−FROZEN20 has visual macro-AP +0.004946 [−0.027460, 0.040927] and F2 −0.009260 [−0.026854, 0.005016]. FULL100−FROZEN100 has visual −0.006662 [−0.027982, 0.027078] and F2 +0.005040 [−0.019473, 0.029255]. These are **reported paired task-cluster intervals**: inconclusive, not equivalence. The prior audit's 1,008 scalar checks and four bootstrap rechecks belong to that audit; they were not rerun here.

The complete frozen/donor three-fold, three-seed results remain valid within their recipes: donor F2 pooled effects are negative for seed 42 and positive for the other two, with corresponding conditional intervals. That mixed pattern should not be reduced to a single selected seed. The 20-epoch OOF bank and 100-epoch pilot meta bank contain 3,275 and 788 training-side rows for this outer fold, respectively; comparison across those budgets is not an isolated duration intervention. Neither unchanged-model transfer nor broad target-adaptation superiority is established.
