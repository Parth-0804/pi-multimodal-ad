## Decisions with the greatest effect on the thesis

1. **Give the literature synthesis a visible home in §2.8.** Chapter 2 currently gives the reader the synthesis in prose. The work-level comparison tables are in Appendix A, while Figure 9.2 combines prior work and experimental coverage. Consider one compact Chapter 2 table tracing each SQ from supported finding and conflicting evidence to the gap and the resulting comparison. Use the 37- and 94-work registers and confirm consequential attributions against the original papers. Keep study-level appraisal in Appendix A and the evaluation of what the thesis actually tested in §9.6.
2. **Assign the repeated study results to distinct parts of the argument.** The Abstract, §1.5, §§9.1–9.4 and §§10.1–10.2 all rehearse versions of the five-study result sequence. Consider making §1.5 the rationale for changing datasets, Chapter 9 the comparison across studies, and §10.1 the direct answer to the main question. Keep any displaced numeric detail with its comparator, metric and uncertainty in the empirical chapter or the discussion. Retain the welding holdout qualification wherever the inference depends on it.
3. **Separate definitions, procedures and observations.** Chapter 2 compares how papers handle missingness and corruption (§2.6). Chapter 3 defines the fusion calculation (§3.5). Chapter 4 describes the evaluation design (§§4.6–4.9). Chapter 6 reports execution and results (§§6.7–6.8). Reduce repeated mechanism descriptions by giving each chapter its own role and a precise cross-reference. Preserve the distinctions between a declared outage and a corrupted input, and between outer recording separation and inner prediction-bank reuse.
4. **Move supporting detail only when its route remains visible.** Review the scope of the PHM diagnostic history (§5.8), the REASSEMBLE follow-ups (§6.9) and the MSM development checks (§7.6). Keep the relevant result, limitation and interpretation in the main text. Use the existing Appendix D and evidence locators for detailed supporting material where appropriate. Preserve the producing run, target version, training budget and assessment exposure with any moved material.
5. **Make the comparative answer easy to find.** The positive assembly contrast is for stacking versus its fitted sensor component. The comparison with equal averaging remains unresolved, and the additional probability fit limits attribution to visual information. The other study results and the synthetic corruption tests qualify the claim in distinct ways. Preserve those qualifications in the transition from §§9.2–9.3 through §§10.1–10.3.
