## Closest work must precede the gap argument

**P032, welding attention fusion**, is close prior work for the original application: physical molten-pool camera images and microphone signals, improved CNN branches, bidirectional cross-attention and an artificial-interference comparison. Its own audio-only and image-only variants each report 100% clean accuracy, equal to its fused result (PDF p18, Tables1–2; p22, Table3). The reported 93.33% under interference is therefore not evidence of an incremental clean fusion gain. The interference operator and exposure matching are insufficiently specified, the nominal test set is60 examples, and the acquisition count conflicts:600 paired sets total onp12 versus600 examples per class/modality onp15. Weld-run-disjoint assessment is not established. These limitations warrant qualified use, not omission of this close welding study.

**P029, thermal-camera plus vibration diagnosis**, provides direct physical camera–machine-sensor late-fusion evidence. Separate CNN predictions feed weighted Dempster–Shafer fusion. The clean diagnostic result is100%, compared with99.06% for IRT CNN and96.32% for vibration CNN (p7, TableVII). It tests Gaussian noise and synthetic vibration bias/stuck faults (pp7–10). The main evidence weights are fixed at0.5; a test-data weight grid is not a learned online quality estimator. Tenfold sample CV does not establish independence between physical runs/bearings. Stuck measurements are corrupted present inputs, not complete modality absence. This paper rules out broad claims that camera/sensor late fusion or sensor-fault stress evaluation has not been studied.

**P028, HANDOVER**, is particularly relevant to adapted robotic failure classification. It compares late/intermediate video–F/T–gripper fusion and action-aware temporal models under an official participant-disjoint337/101/151 split. Video-only I3D achieves64.8±3.3% accuracy, video+F/T I3D-D69.1±1.5%, and all-three I3D-D67.9±3.3% (p7, TableIII). The extra stream is not uniformly beneficial. It also tests cross-robot transfer. The authors explicitly state that their baselines classify the outcome only after a complete trial; causal online prediction is future work (p7). The reported dispersion is over five trainings, not a participant-sampling confidence interval. Published589-trial inventory must remain separate from a thesis implementation’s filtered587-trial cohort.

**P037, fuzzy regularization**, is a close camera/robot-telemetry corruption precedent, but entirely simulated in Gazebo. It fuses one camera view and overlapping20-step telemetry windows for pose regression. The random75/25 sample split does not establish independent trajectory assessment. Tables report means of minimum test losses/maximum accuracies observed across training, so they are not ordinary final held-out means. At five layers/200units, mean minimum MSE is1.326×10^-3 without regularization and0.977×10^-3 with it (p5, TableII). It demonstrates a useful simulation mechanism, not physical industrial fault diagnosis or calibrated field reliability.

**P021, OmniFuser**, directly combines physical tool images and cutting-force data with process-period embeddings, cross-attention and gates. It must be discussed before any claim that this combination is absent. Its evaluation lacks a sufficiently explicit split/group contract for strong cross-tool inference. Training modality dropout and test masking are asserted in discussion without a quantified missingness table. Its headline consistency claim also needs qualification: at Mudestreda horizon12, accuracy is0.886 versus0.897 for TimePFN and0.934 for TimesFM (p11, TableV). This is a benchmark counterexample, not an isolated causal test that adding images harmed a fixed sensor model.

## Reliability and missingness are different evidential claims

**P026, TC-MAF**, contains genuine missing-3D inference evidence: full I-AUROC/P-AUPRO0.9788/0.9901 versus0.9330/0.9586 with3D unavailable (p7, Table3). It also compares the same evidence sources under static pixel mean, score/rank fusion, a learned logistic gate, and a bounded disagreement rule. It is important positive evidence for constrained reliability-aware fusion. Its physical inputs are RGB and geometry; the multimodal detector, RGB reconstruction model and consistency map are evidence branches, not three independent sensors. Eyecandies evaluation uses target normal-data statistics/memory, so it does not establish frozen zero-shot machinery transfer. Repository comments report ACM MM2026 acceptance, while the supplied preprint still has an obsolete Conference’17 header; use the actual version identity rather than that template text.

**P033, disagreement-guided scoring**, supplies valuable mixed evidence. The proposed add-on improves its local MVTec baseline from0.779 to0.800 image AUROC, while the published M3DM reference is0.945 (p10, Table4). On Eyecandies, image AUROC decreases0.810→0.794 although pixel AUROC improves0.963→0.971 and AU-PRO0.849→0.870 (p11, Table5). This supports endpoint- and dataset-dependent behavior. Score consistency is not a demonstrated physical quality sensor; there is no explicit camera-noise or complete-absence stress test in the inspected experiments.

**P038, geometry consistency fusion**, improves its normal-data benchmark and module ablations, but Eq8 specifies a learned scalar α=σ(ω), without an explicit sample-quality input. Its “dynamic” terminology therefore does not establish sample-conditioned reliability adaptation. Table8 evaluates intermediate responses, not independently optimized unimodal models; Table9 reports complete-model I-AUROC0.971 and AUPRO@1%0.493 (p10). P039 is the same binary, not additional evidence.

**P027, audio–vibration–current fusion**, reports missing-input gains and modality dropout, including89.2% without vibration versus65.4% for concatenation (p8, Table3). However, it does not specify an auditable train/validation/test/group partition or repeated-run uncertainty for its custom dataset, and its late missingness visualization is described as conceptual. Retain the source and claims in the audit, but use it as low-confidence background rather than a strong quantitative foundation for SQ3. Do not infer scientific falsity merely from incomplete reporting.

## Defensible synthesis for the chapter

The expanded corpus supports a comparative design need, not a universal literature-absence or first-architecture claim. Real studies already combine industrial cameras with acoustic or numerical machine measurements, compare fusion families, and intervene on noisy or unavailable inputs. Some reliability-aware methods have positive controlled comparisons; others show equal unimodal performance, negative input additions, dataset-dependent scoring losses, simulation-only validation or unclear grouping.

The thesis can therefore contribute a transparent, task-bounded comparison under its own declared observation windows, groups, modality definitions, fitting access and uncertainty conventions. It must demonstrate any claimed advantage over strong unimodal and exposure-matched static baselines. Existing architecture logic, fallback rules, changing gates and attention maps cannot substitute for that outcome evidence. No identified omission here proves that a regime is universally unstudied, and no cited result establishes that the thesis implementation has closed a gap before its own evidence is checked.
