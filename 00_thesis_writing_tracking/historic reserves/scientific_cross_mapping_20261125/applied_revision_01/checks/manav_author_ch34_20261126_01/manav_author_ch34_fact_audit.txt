Minimal scientific corrections identified

- Chapter 4 figure caption: delete “then” from “PHM then provided” so it does not contradict the body chronology that PHM was already under way during the renewed Intel audit.
- Chapter 4 scope decision: retain that the supervisor agreement was reported by the researcher; project records do not constitute independent supervisor confirmation.
- Chapter 3 statistical maximum: describe the highest value in the resampled force channel, rather than promising preservation of the highest native measurement.
- Chapter 3 boundary pooling: specify the last four of the sixteen sampled frames.
- Chapter 3 F2 coefficients: qualify constancy as applying within each fitted model; separately fitted folds can have different coefficient maps.
- Chapter 3 camera selection: remove “therefore” if it presents the absence of failure-location boxes as a documented historical reason for the exact backbone choice. Execution is verified; that exact causal selection history is not.
- Chapter 3 frame-order example: mathematical averaging is order-invariant, but “exactly” can overstate bitwise floating-point identity. A rounding qualification is precise; no reverse-order experiment was run.
- Chapter 3 sample efficiency: replace unsupported language about requiring relatively few training records with the supported fact that the statistical route does not fit a sequence encoder.
