The scientific argument now follows a connected chain: literature makes the components reasonable candidates; historical protocols establish what was actually selected; controlled comparisons establish bounded suitability; saved predictions clarify incremental ordering; external outcomes delimit applicability; the contributions state what those observations support. Published forecasting, detection or joint-training findings are not used as proof of downstream action-classification mechanisms.

Quantitative evidence is retained in the discussion and conclusion. REASSEMBLE F2 improves pooled failure AP over sensors by .0179 [95% CI .0086, .0283], while its .0016 difference from averaging remains unresolved [−.0076, .0112]. The visual term improves within-fold AUROC in all five folds and AP in four. Longer PatchTST training adds .0297 [.0122, .0451], but does not close the gap to statistics; combining the sensor representations adds .0110 [.0054, .0181]. Boundary pooling adds .0885 [.0424, .1293] to visual AP with unchanged head size. These are separate assessed interventions.

The external discussion uses **MSM abnormal-cutting AP**, **REASSEMBLE failure AP** and **ViFailback both-class macro AP** explicitly. MSM's F2-minus-logistic contrast is −.0717 [−.1004, −.0515], conditional on the saved fits and five acquisition dates. Dataset prevalence and different grouping prevent interpreting absolute AP values as a common leaderboard. HANDOVER's failed participant-OOF eligibility gate remains distinct from a measured negative fusion result.

The November 24 ViFailback closure contains 79 of 111 completed fits: complete frozen/donor coverage, four FULL20 fits supporting seed 42/outer fold 0, and three hundred-epoch pilots. Thirty-two FULL20 fits remain unfinished; corruption assessment was not executed. The completed F2 adaptation contrasts are −.009260 [−.026854, .005016] for FULL20 and +.005040 [−.019473, .029255] for FULL100 against their own frozen controls. Their intervals are inconclusive, not evidence of equivalence; their different meta-fitting banks prevent isolating training duration.
