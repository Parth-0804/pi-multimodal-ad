4.6. Limitations.

Feature fusion. Both cable gland and foam perform poorly
for all depth-based methods (Tab. 3 and SM). While the
anomalies in these classes are easier to detect by using color
than by using 3D (see Fig. 1), we expected the fusion of
both modalities to improve performance. Unfortunately, for
