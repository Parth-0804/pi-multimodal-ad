1. PRIMARY RESEARCH OBJECTIVE
============================================================

Our central research question:

Can the multimodal fusion architecture developed using REASSEMBLE
generalize to independent datasets, and does combining vision
and numerical sensors improve failure/anomaly detection compared
with using either modality independently?

We also want to evaluate:

A. Architecture portability across datasets.
B. Predictive benefit of multimodal fusion.
C. Robustness to missing or degraded modalities.
D. Performance stability across random seeds.
E. Generalization to independent experiments or participants.
F. Limitations caused by modality quality and dataset differences.

Do not assume that fusion will outperform unimodal models.

Negative results are scientifically valid.

Distinguish clearly between:

- Successfully reusing the model architecture.
- Transferring pretrained CNN weights.
- Transferring weights learned on REASSEMBLE.
- Achieving better predictive performance.
- Demonstrating robustness to modality corruption.
