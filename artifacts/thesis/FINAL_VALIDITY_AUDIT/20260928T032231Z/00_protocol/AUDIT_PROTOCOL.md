## Preservation, escalation and stopping

Save source/script hashes and environment before execution; log any audit-only code correction as a new revision, never rewrite historical outputs. No project metric/report wrappers imported by the numerical checker. Reuse reported architecture/feasibility evidence and prior controls, labelling it reported/source-inspected rather than newly recomputed. New work is metrics/bootstrap, identity/scope checks, bounded metadata logits, task-boundary checks, sensitivities and review preparation. CPU1 for this process only; no environment upgrades, downloads, deep training or queue changes.

Unexplained material numerical discrepancy or suspected assessment leakage: preserve the finding, identify affected claims, stop dependent predictive interpretation. Continue independent preservation/review preparation only where safe. Expected annotation semantics/human uncertainty are not automatically training leakage. Final status AUTOMATED VALIDITY AUDIT COMPLETE — HUMAN REVIEW PENDING if automated tasks finish without a material blocker and actual reviewer responses remain outstanding. Stop after the required final artifacts.
