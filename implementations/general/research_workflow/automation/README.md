identities. They document past work or proposed stages; they do not authorize
executing historical commands. Source-path and packaging updates remain unfinished;
this navigation does not establish a runnable automation setup.

execution unless the researcher explicitly reauthorizes that specific experiment.
The saved queue uses named stage IDs to distinguish it from other task numbering.
