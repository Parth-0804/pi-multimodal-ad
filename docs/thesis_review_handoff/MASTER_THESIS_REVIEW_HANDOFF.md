## 3. Thesis in one page

The repository studies when process-aware multimodal industrial prediction is justified by trustworthy targets, independently informative modalities, complementary errors and robust evaluation. The repository Main-RQ proxy is “How can a process-aware multimodal AI architecture be designed and evaluated for robust industrial anomaly detection?” SQ1 concerns representations, SQ2 fusion complexity, SQ3 unavailable/degraded modalities, SQ4 calibration, interpretation/modularity and cost. **Approved original wording and supervisor acceptance NOT VERIFIED**: [docs/intel_welding/research_contract.md](../../docs/intel_welding/research_contract.md), [docs/thesis/EXPOSE_TO_FINAL_THESIS_MATRIX.md](../../docs/thesis/EXPOSE_TO_FINAL_THESIS_MATRIX.md).

The two retained core studies answer different tasks. PHM uses post-run gear photos to construct a provisional continuous image score and predicts it from sensor summaries across experiments. Its v3 target covers 24 teeth/run and its evaluation has 20 run units. REASSEMBLE uses completed annotated pick/insert/remove/place segments, hand-camera frames and 22 sensor channels: 4,530 segments, 509 failures, 148 recording groups. Failure is positive; this is retrospective classification. Evidence: [docs/thesis/TARGET_VERSION_LEDGER.md](../../docs/thesis/TARGET_VERSION_LEDGER.md), [runs/phm2026_loeo_evaluation/20260912T110848985882Z-e1911d4f/reports/loeo_summary.json](../../runs/phm2026_loeo_evaluation/20260912T110848985882Z-e1911d4f/reports/loeo_summary.json), [artifacts/reassemble/reports/FINAL_REASSEMBLE_HANDOFF.md](../../artifacts/reassemble/reports/FINAL_REASSEMBLE_HANDOFF.md), [artifacts/thesis/FINAL_VALIDITY_AUDIT/20260928T032231Z/05_final_review/FINAL_VALIDITY_AUDIT_HANDOFF.md](../../artifacts/thesis/FINAL_VALIDITY_AUDIT/20260928T032231Z/05_final_review/FINAL_VALIDITY_AUDIT_HANDOFF.md).

The image reference is privileged/offline and not LOEO. ## 10. Evaluation methodology

PHM uses run-level MAE/RMSE in score percentage points and Spearman, with 10,000 run-cluster draws and three held-out-experiment folds. One remaining experiment trains and the other validates; no held-out-experiment fit. Stored paired points are bootstrap means, not the direct displayed-MAE differences. Fixed-split/image/tooth scores and historical labels remain separate. Sources: [runs/phm2026_loeo_evaluation/20260912T110848985882Z-e1911d4f/reports/loeo_summary.json](../../runs/phm2026_loeo_evaluation/20260912T110848985882Z-e1911d4f/reports/loeo_summary.json) folds/bootstrap/paired_deltas_vs_constant; [scripts/phm2026/results/loeo_evaluation.py](../../scripts/phm2026/results/loeo_evaluation.py) FOLDS/paired_clustered_bootstrap; [docs/thesis/TARGET_VERSION_LEDGER.md](../../docs/thesis/TARGET_VERSION_LEDGER.md). The repository-described organizer-style monotonic diagnostic deliberately fits evaluation labels and is optimistic shape scoring, not leakage-free deployable recalibration: [src/phm2026/evaluation/monotonic.py](../../src/phm2026/evaluation/monotonic.py) monotonic_metrics, fitted_on_evaluation_set.

REASSEMBLE uses AP (sklearn average precision, labelled AUPRC), AUROC, balanced accuracy, macro-F1, failure recall/precision, Brier and 15-bin ECE. Five outer/four inner recording folds are frozen; inner selection, standardization, calibration and thresholds are training-only. Strict branch sub-inner OOF excludes meta-assessment groups before F2 fitting. F2 coefficients are logistic coefficients on probabilities, not convex modality weights. Threshold grid .01–.99 maximizes inner balanced accuracy with tie nearest .5. Source: [src/reassemble/section1_models.py](../../src/reassemble/section1_models.py) evaluate_model/calibrate; [src/reassemble/section1b.py](../../src/reassemble/section1b.py) fit_branch/fit_stacker/guard_partition; [src/reassemble/section1_report.py](../../src/reassemble/section1_report.py).

Paired uncertainty uses 2,000 recording-cluster draws conditional on saved model predictions; seeds do not count as more independent recordings. Structured permutation uses cyclic chronological shifts within recording/action and reruns inner selection; 19 nulls yield minimum plus-one p=.05. Fold/seed tables, per-action effects and recording-balanced weighting are not interchangeable with pooled segment estimates. Audit operating points and metadata-only D0/D1/D2 diagnose shortcut/task-boundary issues, not causal feature attribution: [artifacts/thesis/FINAL_VALIDITY_AUDIT/20260928T032231Z/05_final_review/FINAL_VALIDITY_AUDIT_HANDOFF.md](../../artifacts/thesis/FINAL_VALIDITY_AUDIT/20260928T032231Z/05_final_review/FINAL_VALIDITY_AUDIT_HANDOFF.md), [runs/reassemble/20260927T175336Z-section1b/assessment/results.json](../../runs/reassemble/20260927T175336Z-section1b/assessment/results.json), [runs/reassemble/20260927T192852Z-section2/assessment/results.json](../../runs/reassemble/20260927T192852Z-section2/assessment/results.json). Boundary follow-up reports five AP Bonferroni percentile contrasts; later extension intervals otherwise retain exploratory/marginal qualifications: [artifacts/reassemble/reports/BOUNDARY_AWARE_VISUAL_POOLING_HANDOFF.md](../../artifacts/reassemble/reports/BOUNDARY_AWARE_VISUAL_POOLING_HANDOFF.md) §G, [artifacts/reassemble/reports/ADDITIONAL_IMPROVEMENTS_EXECUTED_HANDOFF.md](../../artifacts/reassemble/reports/ADDITIONAL_IMPROVEMENTS_EXECUTED_HANDOFF.md).
