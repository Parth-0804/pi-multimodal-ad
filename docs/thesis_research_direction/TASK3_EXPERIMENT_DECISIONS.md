## Common design rules for any later approval

- Freeze the claim, effect/clean-loss margins, comparator, primary mixture, population/groups and stop rule before further assessment. No current margin or next experiment is approved by this document.
- REASSEMBLE assessment folds have already been repeatedly examined. Wider coverage or a new seed cannot restore pristine test status. Label further results exploratory even when fitting remains group-disjoint.
- Keep parent actions/recordings, branch fits, corruption realizations and paired draws aligned across arms. Never treat augmented rows, repeated faults, seeds or overlapping folds as new independent acquisition units.
- Use recording groups at minimum; escalate to shared session/operator/object or other dependence units only where metadata supports that hierarchy. Document unknown higher-level dependence rather than inventing independence.
- Training-side normalization, selection, calibration and threshold fitting belong to the evaluated procedure. No labels, pristine counterparts, corruption identity or assessment-driven choices may enter deployed quality features.
- A zero-containing interval is inconclusive, not equivalence. Distinguish a narrowly defined statistical support criterion from practical usefulness and multiplicity across secondary claims.
